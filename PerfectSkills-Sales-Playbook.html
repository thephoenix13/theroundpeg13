4. Pre-Call Checklist

Do this for every lead before dialling. 5 minutes of research = 10× better conversation.

  • Must
    Google their business name. Is there a website? Is it live? Note the URL. If no website — that IS your pitch.
  • Must
    Check Google Maps listing. Any reviews? Rating? Do they have photos, hours, a description? Gaps = conversation starters.
  • Must
    Check if site is mobile-friendly. Open on phone. Slow to load? Looks broken? Note it — use it on the call.
  • Good
    Check their social media (Instagram, Facebook). Active or dead? How many followers? Are they running ads?
  • Good
    Run a quick speed test (Google PageSpeed or gtmetrix.com). Score below 50 on mobile = instant talking point for US leads.
  • Good
    Find one specific thing you can compliment. Their specialisation, their reviews, their niche. Personalisation builds rapport.
  • Optional
    Check if they have a WhatsApp Business number. If not — that's an automation opportunity to mention.

Log everything in the tracker before the call: website status, social score, what you noticed. You'll need it for the follow-up message too.

Say this

— Listen. Let them describe the gap themselves. —

After they answer

• "What we do is build a professional website — mobile-first, fast, designed to get people to call or message you. Most clients start seeing new inquiries within the first month."

• "It's a one-time investment of ₹83,000 — delivered in 2–3 weeks, and then it works for you 24/7." — pause —

• "We also offer WhatsApp automation — so when someone messages you, they get an instant reply with your details and a booking link, even at 2am. Fully automatic, nothing for you to manage."

Soft Close

Moving toward next step

• "I'd rather understand your business before sending a proposal. Can we do a 15-minute video call this week — I'll show you what I found about your current online presence and what we'd build for you."

• "No commitment. I'll also send you a few examples of sites we've done for similar businesses before the call."

— Stop talking. Let them respond. Do not fill the silence. —

If they ask for WhatsApp

When they say "send on WhatsApp"

7. Objection Handling

"I already have a website."
That's great — I saw it actually. A lot of businesses have a website but it's not generating leads. Can I ask — how many inquiries do you get through it in a month? (If low → offer the audit. If they don't know → that's the problem.)
"We get most business from referrals."
Referrals are the best leads — but the first thing a referred person does is Google you to verify you're legit. If they land on a weak site or nothing at all, you lose them before they even call. A good website protects your referral pipeline.
"I don't have budget right now."
I understand. Can I ask — what's one new client worth to you? (They give a number). Our website typically helps clients get 2–3 new inquiries in the first month. At $1,000 it pays for itself with one conversion. Would it help to see a couple of examples first?
"We already have someone managing our digital."
That's fair. Out of curiosity — are you happy with the results you're getting from your current site? (If yes → respect it and exit cleanly. If hesitant → offer the free audit as a second opinion.)
"Send me an email / WhatsApp."
Absolutely — I'll send it right now while we're speaking. What's a good number / email? (This is a warm signal. Send the template immediately and follow up in 24 hours.)
"Not interested."
Totally fine. Just out of curiosity — is it the timing, or is digital presence not something you're focused on right now? (One genuine question. If still no — thank them, mark archive, move on. Do not push.)
"That's too expensive." / "Budget nahi hai."
I understand. Can I ask — what's one new client worth to your business? (They give a number.) If the website brings in even one new client, it pays for itself. And the site works for 3–4 years — that's less than ₹70 per day. Would it help to see a couple of examples first?
"I need to think / discuss with partner."
Of course — what's the best way to follow up? Can I send you a short brief over WhatsApp that you can share with them? That way they have the full picture. (Get the commitment to a specific follow-up time.)

8. Post-Call SOP

What you do immediately after hanging up determines whether the lead converts or goes cold.

1

Log the call in the tracker — within 5 minutes

Open LeadScraper-Tracker.xlsx. Update: call outcome (Interested / Follow-up / Not interested / No answer), notes from the call, and next action with date.

2

Send the WhatsApp / email follow-up — within 10 minutes

Do not wait. Send the relevant template (see Section 9) while you're still top of mind. Personalise it with 1–2 specific things from the call.

3

For US leads — send the audit

If you promised an audit, prepare and send it within 24 hours. Use the site speed score, mobile screenshot, missing CTA — make it concrete, not generic.

4

Schedule the follow-up reminder

If no response in 48 hours — send follow-up #2. If no response to that — call again on Day 6. Use the sequence below.

Follow-up Sequence

Day 0Call + immediate WhatsApp/email follow-up Day 2–3WhatsApp follow-up #2 — value add (share a relevant example or insight) Day 6Call back — offer one free fix or the free audit Day 10Final WhatsApp — social proof + soft close Day 14Archive to cold list. Move on.

Do not over-contact. 4 touchpoints over 14 days is the ceiling. After that, they move to cold and you re-approach in 60 days with a different angle.

10. Tracker & CRM

Use LeadScraper-Tracker.xlsx in D:\Projects\LeadScraper\. Every lead must be logged — no exceptions.

Required columns per lead

  • Business name + phone — from the CSV file
  • City + niche — e.g., Pune / Real Estate
  • Website status — None / Weak / OK / Good
  • Call date + outcome — No answer / Not interested / Follow-up / Interested / Proposal sent
  • Notes — what they said, what pain point came up
  • Next action + date — e.g., "Send audit by Aug 21", "Call back Aug 23"
  • Assigned to — which resource is managing this lead

Lead status definitions

🔵 New — Not yet called
🟡 In Progress — Called, follow-up pending
🟠 Proposal Sent — Waiting on decision
🟢 Won — Agreed, payment / kick-off
🔴 Not Interested — Hard no, do not call again
⚫ Archived — No response after full sequence, re-approach in 60 days
