Pattern Detection

Escalation Rule

3 or more consecutive readings trending in the same direction beyond threshold are flagged as a critical trend. Not a single bad reading — a genuine, confirmed deterioration pattern.

"Ramesh's BP has risen each of the last 4 mornings: 128 → 134 → 141 → 148. Flagged as escalating. Recommended: notify doctor before evening round."

Threshold Breach

Immediate Critical Alert

A single reading above an absolute critical limit triggers an immediate alert — regardless of trend. No waiting for a pattern to emerge when a reading is dangerous on its own.

"BP 182/110 logged for Sunita. Critical threshold exceeded. Alert sent to admin and family immediately."

Missed Dose Correlation

Vitals + Medication Linkage

When vitals trend bad and medications were missed in the same period, the AI connects both signals and presents them together, with a plain-language note for the doctor.

"Prakash's blood sugar has risen this week. He also missed his morning Metformin on 3 of the last 5 days. These may be related."

Cross-Resident Anomaly

Ward-Level Pattern Detection

When 3 or more residents in the same ward develop similar new symptoms within 48 hours, the AI flags a possible environmental or systemic issue — before any human would notice the pattern.

"3 residents in Ward B reported nausea and low appetite within 24 hours. Possible common cause. Recommend environmental check."
