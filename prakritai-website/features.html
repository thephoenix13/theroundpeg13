Feature 01

AI Document Analysis

Most Indians leave the clinic with a folder full of papers they don't fully understand. Lab reports, discharge summaries, ECGs, ultrasound findings — each written in clinical shorthand designed for doctors, not families.

Prakrit's AI reads any medical document and translates it into plain, human language in under 7 seconds. Upload a photo from your phone camera — blurry, angled, even folded — and Prakrit will identify the lab, extract every value, flag what's outside the normal range, and explain what it means for your health.

Trained on Indian lab formats from SRL, Thyrocare, Apollo Diagnostics, Metropolis, and Lal PathLabs, the AI understands how Indian labs present values, reference ranges, and units. It handles regional format differences across states too.

  • Accepts lab reports, prescriptions, discharge summaries, X-ray reports, ultrasounds, ECGs, blood panels — any format
  • Plain-language explanation of each value: what's normal, what's borderline, what to watch and what to ask your doctor
  • Zero medical jargon in output — if a term appears, it's immediately explained in the next sentence

Feature 02

Family Health Profiles

In most Indian households, one person manages everyone's health — the daughter who tracks Papa's diabetes, Amma's blood pressure, the child's vaccinations, and their own annual checkup. Until now, that meant four different WhatsApp folders, scattered prescription photos, and mental notes that don't scale.

Prakrit gives every family member their own dedicated health profile — with their own document timeline, medication schedule, AI insights, and emergency card. One account, one login, complete separation of records. No mixing up Papa's metformin schedule with your own vitamin supplements.

The AI can also look across profiles to surface cross-family patterns. Hereditary conditions, similar lab trends, correlated lifestyle factors — Prakrit is the first health platform built for how Indian families actually function.

  • Unlimited family members per account — parents, grandparents, children, spouse, all in one place
  • Each profile has its own timeline, medications, documents, AI chat context, and emergency card
  • Cross-family insights: AI can identify hereditary risk patterns and shared health trends across profiles

Feature 04

Medication Tracking

Most medication reminder apps treat all pills the same. Prakrit understands that Amma takes Metformin before meals, Telmisartan in the evening, and Calcium after lunch, while Papa's Glimepiride must be taken before breakfast, not after. In chronic disease management, these details matter.

Set up each family member's complete medication schedule once, and Prakrit handles the reminders. Track who took what and when. If a dose is missed two or more times in a week, Prakrit sends a gentle alert and logs the missed dose so you can discuss adherence with the doctor.

When a new prescription is uploaded, Prakrit automatically offers to add the new medications to the schedule, extracting the dosage, frequency, and timing instructions directly from the prescription image.

  • Supports complex schedules: morning/evening, before/after meals, alternate days, weekly doses, taper schedules
  • Adherence tracking with weekly summaries — visual log of taken, missed, and skipped doses
  • Smart prescription extraction — upload a new prescription and medications are auto-populated for review

Prakrit's Health Timeline solves this permanently. Every document uploaded, every medication change, every doctor visit logged — all organized chronologically on a single timeline per family member. Scroll back years. See when a diagnosis was made, how values have trended, which treatments were tried.

The AI continuously analyzes the timeline to surface trends: "Papa's HbA1c has improved three months in a row" or "Amma's blood pressure readings are higher than last quarter." One tap to export the complete timeline as a shareable PDF — ready to hand to any new specialist.

  • Chronological view of all health events — lab results, prescriptions, diagnoses, hospitalizations, doctor visits

Feature 06

Proactive Health Insights

Most health apps wait for you to ask questions. Prakrit's Insights engine works constantly in the background — analyzing your family's uploaded documents, medication adherence data, and health timeline — and surfaces what matters before you know to ask.

It connects dots that are hard to see manually: missed doses followed by higher blood sugar readings, a blood pressure trend that's quietly creeping upward over six weeks, a lab test that's overdue based on the last result. These patterns exist in the data — Prakrit simply makes them visible.

Insights are calibrated. Prakrit doesn't raise false alarms or make diagnoses, and it never flags normal variation as a problem. Every insight comes with a clear next step: discuss with your doctor, schedule a retest, or just watch it.

  • Continuous background analysis across medications, lab results, and health events — no manual input required
  • Preventive retest nudges: "Amma's TSH was last checked 8 months ago — time for a retest?"
  • Correlation alerts: "Papa's missed doses last week align with a 12-point blood sugar increase — worth discussing"

Feature 07

Emergency Health Card

In a medical emergency, 90 seconds of delay in communicating critical information can change outcomes. When a paramedic or emergency room doctor needs to know about allergies, chronic conditions, and current medications, the information must be immediately available — not buried in a WhatsApp folder, not locked behind an app that needs Wi-Fi.

Prakrit's Emergency Health Card is a digital summary card for every family member. Blood type, known allergies, chronic conditions, current medications with doses, and primary emergency contacts — compiled automatically from uploaded documents and always kept current.

The card is available offline. One tap on a locked screen (via a widget or shortcut) opens it. It's shareable as a QR code readable by any smartphone camera, no app download required for whoever receives it.

  • Available fully offline — no internet connection required in an emergency situation
  • Shareable as a QR code or direct link — readable without the Prakrit app installed
  • Auto-updated whenever new prescriptions or lab reports are uploaded and verified
