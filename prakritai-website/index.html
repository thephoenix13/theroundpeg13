Got questions?

Questions we hear most often.

Is Prakrit a medical app? Can it diagnose me?
+
No. Prakrit organizes and explains your health information. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe medications, or replace your doctor's advice. It helps you understand your own documents so you can have better conversations with your doctor.
Is my health data safe?
+
Yes. All data is end-to-end encrypted, DPDP Act compliant, and your records are never used to train AI models. Your family's health data belongs to your family — we are only custodians, not owners.
Does Prakrit work without downloading an app?
+
Yes. You can send lab reports directly on WhatsApp and receive plain-language explanations without downloading anything. The full app experience is available on Android and iOS for unlimited uploads, family profiles, medication reminders, and more.
Which languages does Prakrit support?
+
English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Urdu, Odia, Malayalam, and more — 16+ Indian languages in total, with voice input support. You can switch languages at any time.
Can I manage health records for my parents or elderly relatives?
+
Yes. You can create separate family profiles for every family member — parents, children, grandparents — all managed from one account. Each person has their own health timeline, medication reminders, and emergency card.
Is Prakrit free?
+
Yes, core features are free — including document analysis, family profiles, medication reminders, and the emergency card. Premium plans are available for advanced AI insights, unlimited uploads, and priority support.
Does it work with Indian hospital and lab report formats?
+
Yes. Prakrit is specifically trained on Indian medical reporting formats — SRL, Thyrocare, Apollo, Metropolis, Lal PathLabs, and government health documents. It understands Indian abbreviations, units, and terminology.
What documents can I upload?
+
Lab reports, prescriptions, discharge summaries, X-ray reports, ultrasound reports, ECG results, blood reports, urine reports — any medical document as a photo (JPG/PNG) or PDF. We handle both scanned and digital formats.
