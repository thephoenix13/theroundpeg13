Our story

Built by people who live India's health challenges every day.

Prakrit was born from a simple frustration — brilliant Indian families, managing complex health situations, left without the clarity they deserve.

Our mission.

To make health information accessible, understandable, and actionable — for every Indian family, regardless of language, location, or literacy.

India has world-class medical expertise. What it has lacked is a layer between the doctor's report and the patient's understanding. Prakrit.AI is that layer, translating clinical complexity into plain language every family deserves.

Empowerment, not advice

We translate and organize health data to help patients have better conversations with their doctors. We never diagnose or prescribe — ever. That boundary is sacred to us.

Zero hallucinations

Prakrit answers only from documents you have uploaded. It never invents information. If it doesn't know, it says so clearly. Reliability is not a feature — it is the foundation.

India-first, through and through

Trained on Indian medical report formats, designed for Indian languages, and built for how Indian families actually manage health — joint households, multiple generations, one WhatsApp group.

The opportunity

Three forces that have come together right now.

India's digital health moment is here. And it is bigger than most people realize.

🧠

Technology Readiness

Large language models can now understand complex medical documents and explain them accurately. AI costs have dropped 90%+ in three years, making consumer health AI economically viable for the first time.

📱

Market Readiness

India's 700M+ smartphone users are increasingly comfortable with digital health tools. COVID-19 accelerated adoption by years. Families are actively looking for better ways to manage and share health information.

🏛️

Regulatory Readiness

The Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission is building health data interoperability across the country. 90 crore ABHA accounts have been created. India's DPDP Act is maturing the data protection framework.

AI capability, market demand, and government infrastructure are aligned right now. Prakrit.AI is building for this moment.
