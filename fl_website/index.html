Logo System

The Fortune Logistics logo is a dove in circular flight, enclosed in a cyan circular border. The dove body is rendered in sky cyan; the wing and tail feathers carry a soft lavender/periwinkle accent. The wordmark "Fortune Logistics" appears in italic serif below or alongside the mark.

Voice & Tone
How Fortune Logistics Communicates

The brand voice for the website should feel like a reliable logistics partner — confident, direct and professional. Not a multinational shipping corporation, not a brochure listing trucks.

Confident
Not Boastful

State capabilities clearly and with proof. "We move 5,000 MT daily, 365 days a year" — not "We are the best logistics company."

Direct
Not Corporate-Jargon

Short sentences. Tell the prospect what you do and how it helps their business. Avoid phrases like "end-to-end synergistic supply chain solutions."

Solution-led
Not Feature-listing

Lead with the business problem, follow with the Fortune solution. "Got heavy cargo that airlines won't touch? Our ODC trailers handle it."

Reliable
Not Salesy

Every claim on the website must be something Fortune can actually back up — verified fleet count, real client names, measurable SLAs.

Professional
Not Stiff

B2B-appropriate but approachable. WhatsApp CTA is fine. The brand serves businesses, not government tenders — keep it human.

Indian
Not Generic Global

Speak to the Indian B2B buyer. Reference pan-India reach, North/West belt expertise, and the specific context of Indian road logistics.

Write thisNot this
"On-time delivery, guaranteed.""We leverage cutting-edge technology to ensure timely delivery."
"Request a quote in 60 seconds.""Please fill out our enquiry form and we will revert at the earliest."
"Your cargo, tracked door-to-door.""End-to-end supply chain visibility solutions."
"Founded in 2022. Growing fast.""A young and dynamic company with decades of combined experience."
"ODC? We've moved it before.""We offer Over Dimensional Cargo transportation services as per customer requirements."
Brand Usage
Do's and Don'ts

Rules for maintaining brand consistency across the website and all digital communications.

✓ Do

  • Use Fortune Cyan (#00AEEF) as the dominant brand color on all pages
  • Use the bird mark + italic wordmark together as the primary logo
  • Pair dark hero sections (#111827) with cyan CTA buttons for contrast
  • Use Inter as the website typeface — bold weights for headings
  • Include the ISO 9001-2015 certification mark near the logo
  • Use amber (#FFC107) only as a small accent — badges, highlights
  • Always verify operational numbers before publishing as proof points
  • Keep sufficient white space — don't crowd the layout
  • Use real fleet photos and operations imagery wherever possible

✕ Don't

  • Don't change the cyan to a different blue or teal without client sign-off
  • Don't stretch, rotate, recolor or add effects to the logo
  • Don't place the logo on a busy photographic background without a background card
  • Don't use the italic serif wordmark font for body or heading text
  • Don't publish the "850+ vehicles" or "30,000+ clients" claims — these are from Best Roadways, not confirmed as Fortune's own data
  • Don't use the "established 1986" date — Fortune was founded in 2022
  • Don't use client logos without written permission from each client
  • Don't use amber or orange as the primary color on any page section
  • Don't use stock photography of generic foreign trucks — use Indian fleet imagery
