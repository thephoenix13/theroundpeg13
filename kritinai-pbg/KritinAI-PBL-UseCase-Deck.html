How PBL's warehouse operates today.

The Excel situation — what's happening across PBL's sites.

Warehouse daily stock logs → separate Excel files at each site

Decisions are reactive. A stockout discovered in the morning report happened yesterday. The MRO spare part that ran out was already missing from the line.

👤

One person holding it together

When the person who does the morning aggregation is absent, the report doesn't exist. The process is undocumented and person-dependent.

Use Case 3 · Production Floor

From paper shift logs to a live OEE dashboard — in 6 weeks.

The Situation

Line operators log production output on paper at shift end. Quality checks are done verbally and noted by QA on a clipboard. OEE is calculated the next morning when someone manually enters MES printouts into an Excel template. By the time the plant manager sees the numbers, the shift is history. No corrective action is possible. Patterns in defects, downtime, and underperformance are only visible in weekly reviews — far too late.

What we'd build — 3 components
①

Production Logging App — Canvas App

Line operators tap output, downtime reason (coded list), and quality check result from a shared tablet at the machine. 60 seconds. Timestamped and shift-tagged automatically.

②

Real-Time Threshold Alerts — Power Automate

If OEE drops below 80%, fill level variance exceeds ±2%, or carbonation check flags out-of-spec — alert fires to line manager and QA supervisor mid-shift, while there's still time to act.

③

Live OEE Dashboard — Power BI

Plant manager sees output vs. target, downtime by reason and machine, and quality rate by shift and line — refreshed every 15 minutes. Available on desktop, mobile, and boardroom screen.

Today
Paper production log at shift end — re-entered into Excel
OEE calculated next morning from MES printout
Quality issues discovered in weekly QA review
Downtime reasons inconsistently logged — no pattern visible
Cross-line comparison requires manual consolidation
With KritinAI
Operator logs output and quality at the machine — live in Dataverse
OEE visible in Power BI within 15 minutes — during the shift
Quality alert fires mid-shift — corrective action still possible
Downtime reasons from a standardized dropdown — clean data, visible patterns
All lines compared on one dashboard — no manual consolidation

This is Power Apps and Power BI as one system — not two separate projects. One Power Apps developer with ETL knowledge builds and maintains both streams.
