Day 0 — license-free features
Seven features that ship before FIU, before DSA, before anything. Each one builds the habit that makes the licensed product stickier on launch day.
No FIU No DSA No IRDAI No AMFI

These are not filler features to keep the team busy before FIU clears. Five of the seven become revenue-earning the moment a license comes through — no rebuild, just a new CTA or data source. Ship them early, get user feedback, and the licensed product launches into a base of people who already understand the tool. Regulatory basis: SEBI IA Regulations 2013 (Reg. 4 — general information exemption), RBI FREE-AI Framework August 2025 (applies to regulated entities only, not pure software products), DPDPA 2023 (data consent — required regardless).

1. Financial health quiz (self-reported score)

Ten to twelve questions: income bracket, active EMIs, months of emergency fund, investment types, insurance coverage. User gets a score out of 100 with a category breakdown. Last screen: "Connect your accounts to get your real score."

Gateway to: AA consent. Every user who finishes the quiz is already thinking about their score — the logical next step is connecting accounts to see the real number.

Design guardrail: Category-level gaps only in output ("emergency fund: low"). No product names. This keeps it within SEBI IA Reg 4's educational exemption.

2. AI document summarizer (user-uploaded PDFs)

User uploads their own bank statement, loan agreement, or insurance policy. AI returns a plain-language summary: key numbers, dates, hidden charges, terms. This is not AA — the user hands over their own file directly. No RBI or SEBI touch.

Gateway to: Full AA product. Uploading a document is the same mental step as granting AA consent. Users who do one convert to the other at much higher rates.

Regulatory basis: RBI FREE-AI Framework applies only to regulated entities (banks, NBFCs). A software product processing a user's own file is outside that scope. DPDPA 2023 consent language is needed in onboarding.

3. Tax optimizer (80C, 80D, HRA, NPS, old vs. new regime)

User enters income, current investments, HRA details. App shows tax liability under both regimes, optimal 80C allocation, and how much they can still save before March 31. One of the highest-search-volume personal finance queries in India, every January through March.

Gateway to: AMFI ARN (MF referrals). Surface ELSS category at the end — informational, not a buy call, so no ARN needed yet. The same screen earns commission once ARN is live.

4. Manual budget + expense tracker

User logs income and expenses by category. Monthly snapshot, spending trends, savings rate. No license — expense tracking apps run freely in India. High daily engagement driver, builds the muscle memory for interacting with the app.

Gateway to: AA dashboard. When AA goes live, reconcile manual entries against actual transactions. "You thought you spent ₹12,000 on dining. You actually spent ₹18,400." That gap is the product's most persuasive moment.

5. Credit card comparison + affiliate links

Public card features by spending type: reward rate, annual fee, fuel surcharge waiver, lounge access. User applies via affiliate link — EarnKaro or BankBazaar pay ₹500–3,000 per approved card. No DSA needed; this is referral, not loan arrangement.

Gateway to: DSA cross-sell. Once DSA is active, pair each card recommendation with a personal loan offer on the same screen.

Only Day 0 feature with a dependency: Pvt Ltd registration needed to open the affiliate account. Unblocked by Month 0, Week 3–4.

6. Educational AI chatbot (concepts only, no specific picks)

Answers general finance questions: "What is a SIP?", "How does home loan prepayment work?", "Explain Section 80C." SEBI's January 2025 finfluencer guidelines are explicit — educational content is outside IA registration requirements. The chatbot stays on mechanics and concepts, never names a fund or stock to buy.

Gateway to: AI Financial Advisor (Module 2). Same interface, same UX. The upgrade happens when the user connects their accounts — the chatbot then knows their actual situation. "You asked about home loan prepayment — based on your HDFC loan at 9.1%, here is what prepaying ₹1L today saves you."

Three rules that keep it legal: no specific security or fund name in outputs, no price data under 3 months old, no future performance claims. These are the exact lines SEBI's Reg. 4 exemption draws.

Module 3 — Loan Suite (launches Month 7)

Launch trigger: 1,000 active dashboard users. Uses AA data already flowing in — no manual entry.

  • All loans in one view: personal, home, vehicle, education, credit card outstanding
  • EMI calendar — upcoming payment dates across all lenders
  • Prepayment calculator: "Pay ₹50K extra today, save ₹1.4L in interest, close 8 months early"
  • Refinancing alert: "SBI offering 8.5% — your current rate is 9.2%. Switch saves ₹3,200/month"
  • Debt payoff prioritizer: which loan to close first by rate, tenure, and cash flow
  • Loan consolidation: detect multi-loan situations, calculate savings, refer to DSA partner for commission (1–1.5% of new disbursement)

Module 4 — Financial Health Score (launches Month 8)

Launch trigger: Loan Suite at 1,000 users. Finlyft AI's proprietary score — not CIBIL.

  • 300–900 scale (mirrors CIBIL for intuitive reading)
  • 6 components: credit utilisation (20%), debt-to-income (25%), savings rate (20%), emergency fund coverage (15%), investment diversification (10%), repayment track record (10%)
  • Month-on-month trend with explanation
  • "What's dragging your score" — specific breakdown
  • Actionable fix: "Pay Axis card below 30% utilisation — adds ~40 points"
  • Premium gate: Free users see the number. Paid users see full breakdown. First subscription revenue.

FLM — where the Year 2 moat comes from

  • Year 1: Frontier LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google). Fast, high quality, no ML team needed.
  • The problem at scale: 5,000 MAU × 5 queries/day × ₹1/query = ₹7.5L/month in API costs. That is the entire Month 12 revenue target, wiped out.
  • Year 2, Q1: Fine-tune Llama 3.2 or Sarvam-2B on Finlyft AI's 50,000+ example dataset. One-time GPU cost: ₹2–5L on AWS SageMaker.
  • Year 2, Q2: Self-host on AWS. Cost drops to ₹20–40K/month vs. ₹75K–1.5L on APIs. Break-even at 2,500–3,000 MAU.
  • The moat: A model trained on real Indian user financial queries — PPF, NPS, ELSS, Section 80C, Hindi-English code-switching — cannot be replicated without the same data and the same domain time. AA integration is replicable. This is not.
