The Problem
The average urban Indian has money in 4–6 institutions and has never seen it all in one place. They have loans they've never tried to optimize, investments they don't track across platforms, and credit cards they don't know the combined utilisation of.
  • A CFP charges ₹2,000–8,000/hour — most can't afford it
  • Loan consolidation could save thousands — nobody tells them
  • Investment XIRR is split across Groww, Zerodha, PPF, NPS
The Solution
Finlyft AI uses RBI's Account Aggregator (AA) framework — a consent-based, read-only data pipe — to pull every financial account a user has into one screen. An AI advisor with full context of their actual numbers gives specific, actionable advice in Hindi and English.
  • One AA consent tap — no manual data entry, ever
  • AI that knows your accounts before you ask the first question
  • Specific: "Pay your HDFC card off — saves ₹14,000 this year"
